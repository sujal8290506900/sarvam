    How candidates are scored

    Each source is scored 0–1 on four factors and combined:
    0.40 × distance (nearer is better, 300 km horizon)  +  0.30 × quantity fit (available ÷ needed)  +  0.15 × urgency  +  0.15 × availability.
    ETA = straight-line distance at 40 km/h plus 20 min loading. Stock is reserved while a dispatch is active and only decremented on delivery, so no pallet is promised twice. A human coordinator stays in charge and can override any recommendation.
